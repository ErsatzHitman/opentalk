# Preferences

## Appearance
  seg #1 Theme: Light | Dark | (Auto)

## Dictation
  [x] #2 Dictation sounds -- Play a short cue when recording starts and stops.
  [ ] #3 Pause media while dictating -- Pause music and video when you start, resume afterwards.
  [x] #4 Mute system audio while dictating -- Silence your speakers while you record so nothing leaks into the microphone.
  [x] #5 Automatic pasting -- Paste the text into the app you were using. When off, it is only copied.
  [ ] #6 Keep transcription in clipboard -- Leave the text on the clipboard instead of restoring what you had copied.
  [x] #7 Smart spacing -- Add a space before the text when it continues a sentence.

## Floating icon
  [ ] #8 Auto-hide when idle -- Show the icon only while recording or transcribing.
  seg #9 Start position: (Bottom right) | Bottom center | Bottom left
  [x] #10 Show app bubble -- A round icon of the app you are dictating into, beside the indicator.

## Language
  choice #11 Transcription language > Auto-detect
  note: Auto-detect works for most speech. Pick a language for better accuracy.

## Startup
  [ ] #12 Launch at login -- Start OpenTalk in the tray when you sign in to Windows.

## Microphone
  choice #13 Input device > System default
  choice #14 Keep microphone warm > Off
  note: Keeping the stream open after a dictation makes the next start instant, but the Windows microphone indicator stays on.
