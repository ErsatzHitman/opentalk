# Cleanup & AI

## AI rewrite
  note: Say a trigger phrase at the end of a dictation. The text before it is rewritten concisely by your AI provider and pasted instead.
  [x] #1 Enable AI rewrite -- Uses the AI provider below, even when AI cleanup is off.
  field #2 Trigger phrase [text] = "rewrite this concisely" (hint "rewrite this concisely", max 80)
  button #3 Reset to default <Link> (disabled)
  note: Pick words you would not say otherwise; leave it empty to turn the rewrite off. Avoid digits and filler words such as "um".
  banner[warn]: No AI model is set up yet. Add an API key and a model under AI provider below, or the rewrite pastes your plain text.

## On-device cleanup
  note: Always on and instant: runs on your device before anything is pasted.
  seg #10 Filler word removal: Off | (Standard) | Aggressive
  note: Removes um, uh, stutters and false starts. Aggressive also drops "basically", "actually" and similar.
  [x] #11 Fuzzy dictionary correction -- Fix near-misses of your dictionary words, for example "open whisper" to "OpenWhispr".
  [ ] #12 Spoken line breaks -- Turn "new line" and "new paragraph" into real line breaks.

## AI cleanup
  note: An optional language model polishes punctuation and grammar after the on-device pass.
  [x] #20 Enable AI cleanup -- Send the transcript (never the audio) to the model below. It adds a moment of latency; a failure falls back to the on-device result.
