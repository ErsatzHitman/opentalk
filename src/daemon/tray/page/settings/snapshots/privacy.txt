# Privacy & Data

## History
  note: Your dictations are stored only on this PC.
  [x] #1 Save history -- Keep a list of your dictations in History. Turn off to save nothing.
  choice #2 Keep transcripts for > Keep forever
  note: Older entries are deleted automatically.

## Recordings
  note: Audio is never kept on disk.
  [x] #3 Keep the last failed recording -- Hold it in memory so you can retry after a network or key error. Cleared when OpenTalk exits.
  [ ] #4 Save discarded dictations -- Also list dictations that were cancelled or contained no speech, under "Show discarded" in History.

## Your data
  note: Everything lives in <data folder>
  button #10 Open data folder <Outline>
  button #11 Clear history... <Destructive>
