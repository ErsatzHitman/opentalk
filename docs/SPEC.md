## Pill (exact OpenWhispr VoicePill)
- Window 208x120, layered, topmost, no-activate, toolwindow. Placed at work area bottom-right, 4px margin.
  Group anchored 12px from window right and bottom (pill right edge 16px from work-area edge).
- Footprints: idle circle 40x40; recording capsule 98x36. Border 1px inside. rounded-full.
- Capsule internals: pr 6px when compact; logo slot 22x22; divider hidden while recording (margins 3px);
  waveform lane 52x24: 11 bars, 2px wide, 3px gap, min 4 max 22, rounded, color foreground.
- Level (changed in round 3, owner: "smoothly responds to my voice in real time and stays stable when I'm silent"):
  the capture thread runs a streaming meter (`audio/meter.rs`): 70 Hz high-pass (DC removal), power of the latest
  20 ms every 10 ms, an adaptive noise floor in dB (snaps down, creeps up; -66..-38 dBFS), a gate with hysteresis
  (opens above max(floor+9 dB, -48 dBFS), closes after 150 ms below max(floor+5 dB, -52 dBFS)) and a display
  level 0..1 linear in dB between the close threshold and a slowly decaying running speech peak (-34..-10 dBFS),
  so quiet and hot microphones both use the whole range. A closed gate publishes exactly 0.0. It publishes the
  level and the highest level since the pill last looked (`take_peak`), so nothing between two frames is lost.
  Bar = 4 + level*18 (the old `(rms*8)^0.75` mapping and the 1/255 px "quiet" band are gone). The bars are a tapped
  delay line over one continuous envelope: the envelope follows max(level, peak) every frame with a 12 ms attack and
  a 120 ms release (integrated exactly, frame-rate independent); the right-most bar is the live envelope, bar j
  shows the envelope (10-j)*80 ms ago, interpolated between frames, so the lane scrolls smoothly instead of
  changing every 80 ms. Silence is exactly flat: every bar 4px, frames skipped, timer slow (the capture thread
  wakes it when the gate opens). Reset to 4px on start. Look unchanged (11 bars, 2px, 3px gap, 4-22px, colours,
  660 ms fade-in). `--render-pill --level x` is the display level (0 = flat, 1 = full height), not an RMS.
- Logo (24 viewBox at 22px): ring circle c(12,12) r10 stroke 2; bars x=8.75 y10..14, x=12 y8..16, x=15.25 y10..14, stroke 2 round caps.
- States/colors (light | dark):

  thinking (transcribing): 40x40, border/60, fg, signal glow on.
  dark only shadow: 0 1px 3px rgba(0,0,0,.2).
- Entrance: 0-260ms circle w/ recording colors; 260ms expand to 98x36 over 300ms cubic-bezier(0.2,0,0,1);

## Pill surface material (liquid glass)
Implements the `liquid-glass` skill for a layered window (no DWM backdrop is available): `src/daemon/pill/glass.rs` (pure) and `capture.rs` (Win32).
- Surfaces: pill capsule/circle, the detached cancel button and the app-icon bubble (glass circles, same recipe, same height as the pill), the tooltip (menu tier) and the error card (modal tier). The logo, waveform bars, cancel glyph, divider, hover gradient and text are drawn on top, unchanged.
- Backdrop: the screen rect under the window plus a refraction margin (`0.12 * monitor size`, capped at 96 logical px, clipped to the monitor) is copied with `BitBlt` from the screen DC, box-averaged into 3 px cells, frosted (3-pass box blur ~ gaussian sigma 10 logical px, `saturate(125%)`) and sampled bilinearly (edges clamp).
- Refraction: the skill's field verbatim (`GLASS_PRESET`: edge 0.015 / rim 0.028 / base 0.05, distances 0.5 / 1.7 / 0.2, corner boost 0.06, ripple 0.26, **warp off**), signed rounded-rect `distPx`, texcoord-space normal, `pageW x pageH` = the pill's monitor in logical px. Each glass pixel samples the frosted backdrop at its displaced position. The displacement map of an exact rounded box is baked once per (size, radius, monitor size, scale, sub-pixel phase) and reused, but only for a shape seen twice in a row at the same device position (tracked per shape, 8 shapes): a frame of the expand / shrink, of the cancel button's emerge / retract or of the bottom-right slide is evaluated inline from depth / corner tables and never bakes or evicts a map.
- Material on top (tokens of `liquid-glass.css`): frost layer (the modal tier, stronger for the 36 px pill: white 56 % in light mode, near-black 69 % in dark mode; the pill follows the app theme), tint gradient white -> 70 % grey at 6 % (11 % while hovered), three inset rim highlights (top 1 px at 0.55 / 0.50, hairline 0.12 / 0.10, bottom 0.06), 1 px border (`rgba(30,33,45,.12)` / `rgba(255,255,255,.08)`; x0.6 in the processing / thinking / unavailable states, as the old `border/60`), drop shadow (the skill's `0 10px 30px` scaled to the 12 px the window leaves around the pill: `0 3px 9px` light, `0 4px 10px` dark; lifted while hovered).
- Legibility (unit-tested): foreground against the glass is >= 4.5:1 over the waveform lane and the logo for pure white, pure black and a busy stripe/noise backdrop, in both themes.
- Cost (PLAN section 1 stays binding: an idle daemon runs no timers and does not poll): nothing is captured while the window is hidden. The copy (`BitBlt` waits for the compositor, ~5 to 25 ms wall but <0.5 ms CPU) and the cell hash / frost run on a worker thread (`pill-glass`, started on the first request, blocked on a channel while idle, joined on exit) so the UI thread never waits: it only posts a request (~10 us) and installs a finished picture on `WM_APP_GLASS`. A periodic USER timer on the pill window requests a capture every 125 ms while the pointer is on the pill or it is dragged, and every 250 ms while preparing, thinking, unavailable (mic reconnecting) or animating; it is **killed whenever the pill rests** (the idle circle, and an error card counting down) and **while it records** (round 3: a screen `BitBlt` waits for the compositor and was a suspect for waveform hitches, so a recording refreshes its backdrop on events only: on show, on a screen event after the 400 ms settle, and once the pill has stopped resizing or moving; its frost can lag a changing screen while recording). `OPENTALK_NO_GLASS_CAPTURE=1` turns the capture off altogether (no display-affinity flag, no `BitBlt`, neutral frost) for A/B comparisons on a real machine. The resting circle and the error card are refreshed on events only, so their frost can be slightly stale: on show (the kept picture is placed at once and refreshed within a frame or two), on a state / size / dock / scale / monitor / position change (rate-limited to one request per 100 ms, one in flight; an owed request becomes a one-shot timer that fires once and is killed), on hover (the 125 ms rate runs while the pointer is on the pill), on `WM_SETTINGCHANGE` (wallpaper, colours), and through out-of-context WinEvent hooks (`SetWinEventHook`, `WINEVENT_OUTOFCONTEXT | WINEVENT_SKIPOWNPROCESS`, no DLL injection; installed while the window is shown, removed when it hides) for `EVENT_SYSTEM_FOREGROUND`, `EVENT_SYSTEM_MOVESIZEEND` and `EVENT_SYSTEM_MINIMIZESTART`/`END`: another window coming to the front, finishing a move / resize, or being minimised or restored posts `WM_APP_SCREEN_EVENT`, which arms a one-shot 400 ms settle timer (the changed window has finished animating; a burst of events costs one capture). These events come at human pace; the chatty `EVENT_OBJECT_LOCATIONCHANGE` / `SHOW` / `HIDE` (carets, cursors, tooltips) are not hooked, so content that scrolls or redraws under a resting circle without a window event is picked up only at the next event or hover. A lost `WM_APP_GLASS` post (full queue) is recovered by the next timer tick, settle timer or screen event. A capture whose cell hash (colours >> 2, so grain and dithering do not count) is unchanged recomposes nothing. Steady recording frames still redraw only the waveform lane: the glass lives in the cached base layer, which is rebuilt only when the backdrop picture changes. The error card uses a stronger frost (white 82 % / near-black 95 %) because its muted text is smaller than the pill's glyphs.
- Memory and CPU budget (unit-tested bounds, `capture_buffers_are_small_and_bounded`, `baked_displacement_cache_is_bounded_in_slots_and_bytes`; numbers below are an optimised build run under Wine on a 4-core Linux host, not a Windows desktop):
  - Buffers: the capture DIB is (208 + 2 x 96) x (120 + 2 x 96) logical px x 4 B = 0.5 MB at 1x, 1.1 MB at 1.5x, 2.0 MB at 2x, and is given back after every event-driven copy (whenever no periodic timer runs) and when the window hides. The cell grid + frost working set is ~0.3 MB (transient, on the worker); the kept frosted picture is ~40 KB. The baked displacement maps are capped at 8 maps / 2 MiB in all (a 98x36 capsule is 0.13 MB at 2x, the 442 px error card 1.5 MB), least recently used first. Expected steady cost on top of the base daemon: ~2.5 MB at 1x, ~5 MB at 2x while recording; ~1.5 MB while resting.
  - Per capture (1x / 2x): screen copy 0.4 / 1.5 ms wall (Xvfb; not measured on Windows, where `BitBlt` also waits for the compositor), cell average + hash 0.3 / 0.4 ms (reads a quarter of the pixels), frost 0.4 / 0.4 ms (only when the picture changed). A static screen therefore costs ~0.3 % (1x) to ~0.8 % (2x) of one core while thinking at 4 Hz and nothing while resting or recording (one capture per screen event); a constantly changing backdrop (video) adds a base-layer rebuild of 1.0 / 4.0 ms per capture (at 4 Hz: ~1.1 % / ~2.5 % of one core, which is 0.1 to 0.3 % of an 8-core machine, the unit `bench.ps1` reports as `CpuPctAll`). Steady 60 fps recording frames cost 0.02 / 0.07 ms each (waveform lane only, unchanged from before the glass). Expand and shrink frames (a new capsule size every frame) cost 0.6 / 2.3 ms and 1.0 / 3.8 ms median at 1x / 2x (before the table-driven field and shadow: 1.6 / 6.4 ms and 1.7 / 6.5 ms; a displacement map was baked per frame), the base rebuild 0.5 / 2.0 ms (was 0.9 / 3.8 ms). A frame of the cancel button's emerge / retract (pill, bubble and button glass, tooltip, all inline) costs 1.4 / 4.8 ms median. The error card's glass is rebuilt in 7 / 31 ms when a capture finds a new picture under it (it is captured as it appears and then only on screen events).
  - The measurements are printed by `cargo test -- --nocapture capture_cost glass_frame_cost glass_paint_cost` and, on a real desktop, by the ignored `desktop_pill_capture_cost`.
- Capture exclusion: the window calls `SetWindowDisplayAffinity(WDA_EXCLUDEFROMCAPTURE)` so the screen copy never contains the pill. **Side effect: the pill (and bubble, tooltip, error card) is invisible to screenshots, screen recordings and screen sharing.** The flag needs Windows 10 2004; where it is refused, or when a capture fails (secure desktop, locked session), nothing is captured and the glass is frosted over a flat neutral backdrop (light `#d9dce3`, dark `#30333a`) - never garbage. The exclusion itself (that `BitBlt` with `CAPTUREBLT` honours the flag) is checked by the ignored `desktop_pill_capture_excludes_the_pill`, which needs an unlocked, composed desktop and skips itself otherwise.
- Goldens: `--render-pill <state> <t_ms> out.png [--dark] [--composite] [--upscale N] --backdrop white|black|gradient|busy-text|path.png` renders over a fixture (`tests/fixtures/pill_backdrops/`, the bottom-right 640x360 of a 1920x1080 screen with a 40 px taskbar, window docked as the daemon would); without `--backdrop` the neutral backdrop is used, so every render is deterministic.
- Known limits: the cancel cross is drawn in `muted-fg` (unchanged) and reaches only ~2.4:1 against the frosted glass over a pure-black backdrop in light mode (and ~2.2:1 over pure white in dark mode); it is a 13 px glyph that turns `fg` under the pointer, and the waveform, logo and all text meet 4.5:1. The error cards' goldens contain GDI text and are only valid on a Windows machine (Wine lacks Segoe UI); they are not regenerated from Wine.
- Pictures: contact sheets of the pill states over the busy-text and gradient fixtures (light left, dark right; idle, the expand at 560 ms, recording, thinking, the detached cancel button, tooltip with the button out, all with the app-icon bubble) are in `docs/screenshots/pill-glass/` (`pill-over-busy-text.png`, `pill-over-gradient.png`, and `error-card-over-backdrops.png`: the card over the gradient, light and dark), rendered with `--composite`; the error card text there is Wine's fallback font. Emerge / retract frames: `docs/screenshots/cancel-detach/`; expand / shrink frames: `docs/screenshots/smooth-anim/`.
- The cancel button paints exactly like the bubble (`Surface` circle with the same shadow, lift, border and rim, `draw_cancel_button`), under the pill so it appears to come out from behind it; its radius changes while it slides, so the displacement map is re-baked per frame during the 280 ms emergence only (a 36 px circle, ~0.03 MB). It lies inside the window, so the backdrop capture region (window + refraction margin) already covers it.
- Superseded tokens: `surface-0/1/3`, `popover`, `border` and `border-hover` no longer paint any fill or border (the glass does); `fg` and `muted-fg` still colour the logo, bars, cancel glyph, text and the Retry button.

## OpenTalk additions
- App bubble: circle left of pill (gap 8), diameter == pill height (animated), same chrome as pill, foreground app icon ~60% of diameter. **It is live, and it is the paste target** (setting `show_app_bubble`; off = none of the following runs):
  - *Instant.* While the daemon runs, one out-of-context `SetWinEventHook(EVENT_SYSTEM_FOREGROUND)` (`WINEVENT_OUTOFCONTEXT | WINEVENT_SKIPOWNPROCESS`, no DLL injected) hands each newly focused window to a single low-priority worker thread that blocks on a channel (no timer, no polling). The worker resolves that app's icon into a small LRU in `appicon` (16 entries, 1 MB cap, keyed by exe path; ~16 KB per entry at 64 px), so the hotkey press finds it already there and the bubble is set in the same call that shows the pill (it fades in with the pill's expansion as before). A miss (an app the worker has not seen, or one that needs the shell-factory icon path) falls back to the old helper thread. The background pre-resolve never uses the shell-factory path (it would map ~35 shell DLLs into the daemon).
  - *Live.* If another app comes to the front while recording or transcribing, the bubble swaps icon and tooltip name with a 180 ms cross-fade (`ICON_SWAP_MS`, ease-out); the bubble itself does not move or resize. Another window of the same app moves the paste target only.
  - *The paste-target rule.* The text goes to the app the bubble shows when the text is ready: the window in front at that moment, **ignoring OpenTalk's own daemon windows (pill, tray menu) and transient shell surfaces** (taskbar and its tray flyouts, Start / Search / Quick Settings hosts, the Alt-Tab and Task View switchers, the input switcher), which keep the previous target. The same rule picks the target at the hotkey press (pressing it with the taskbar focused targets the app you were last in) and for Retry. Focus is returned to that window before the keystroke if it moved. The tray popup (and with it every Settings, History and Dictionary page) is one of OpenTalk's own daemon windows, so it is never a target: dictating while a page is open pastes into the app you were in before (dictating into a popup text field is not supported). The History entry records the app that received the text. With the bubble off the target is simply the window the hotkey went down in, as before.
  - *Cost.* Between focus changes nothing runs. A focus change costs one `PostMessage`-sized callback, one `OpenProcess` + path query and (first time per app) one icon load on the background thread.
- Deterministic filler removal (OpenWhispr only had LLM). Optional LLM cleanup w/ OpenWhispr prompt, 4s timeout, validation.
- Voice rewrite (`src/core/rewrite.rs`, `pipeline::clean_with_calls`, `llm::rewrite`). Config: `rewrite_trigger` (default "rewrite this concisely", empty = off), `ai_cleanup.rewrite_enabled` (default on), `ai_cleanup.rewrite_timeout_ms` (default 10000, 0 = default, clamped to 250..60000 ms, config.json only); all serde-default, so older config files load unchanged.
  - *Detection.* Runs on the text after `process_pre_llm` (fillers, stutters, dictionary and line breaks are done), before any LLM and before snippets. The phrase is cleaned with the same filler mode and language first. Token-aligned, end-anchored, case / punctuation / whitespace / apostrophe insensitive, fullwidth folded, one CJK character per token, no edit-distance fuzz. Only the phrase or one trailing courtesy group (`please`, `thanks`, `thank you`) may end the text; connectives (`and`, `then`, `so`, `please`, `also`) and clause punctuation in front of the phrase are stripped from the body. A phrase needs two tokens and six letters (a CJK character counts double) and at most 80 characters, else the feature is off for the dictation (the Settings page shows the same rule through `Trigger::check`).
  - *Decision.* No match: unchanged (cleanup LLM if enabled). Only the phrase: nothing is pasted, card "Nothing to rewrite". Body of 3 words or fewer: pasted as spoken, no call. Otherwise the body goes to the cleanup provider / model / key (also with AI cleanup off) with its own prompt (`prompt::REWRITE_PROMPT`: keep every fact, never longer, no preamble, the speaker is never talking to the model), temperature 0, dictionary words plus snippet triggers as hints, wrapped in `<transcript>`. The rewrite REPLACES the cleanup call for that dictation; a failed rewrite does not fall back to the cleanup call. Snippets expand last.
  - *Validation* (`validate_rewrite`): thinking / fences / tags / "Here is the concise version:" / quotes stripped; empty, a refusal opener, the transcript said twice, more than 1.05 x the input words + 1, a collapse below 8% of a 8+ word input, or under 20% content-word overlap (answer instead of rewrite; skipped for CJK) are rejected.
  - *Failure.* Any error (no key, timeout, HTTP, network, rejected answer) pastes the deterministic body (phrase removed) and shows an informational pill card (no Retry): "Rewrite timed out", "Check your AI key", "AI provider is rate limiting", "Couldn't reach the AI provider", "Rewrite didn't look right" or "Add an AI key to rewrite". Cards are the ordinary error card (442 px, title 16 px, description 14 px, 3-5 s auto-dismiss); preview states `rewrite-nothing`, `rewrite-failed`, `rewrite-nokey`, pictures in `docs/screenshots/rewrite/`. Card precedence after a delivered dictation: paste problem, rewrite fallback, recording limit.
  - *Cancel.* The pill stays in Thinking for the whole call (mute was released at stop). Esc / the cancel button drop the job; the worker thread returns when the HTTP call does (at most timeout + 300 ms) and its result is discarded, nothing is pasted or stored.
  - *History.* `text` = what was pasted, `raw` = the full recognised speech including the phrase, `rewritten` = true only for a delivered rewrite, `original` = the deterministic body that was rewritten. Old history lines load with `rewritten: false`.
- Ctrl+Win Start-menu suppression (mask key 0xE8), injected-event filtering, Esc cancel.
- Clipboard: full format snapshot/restore, excluded from clipboard history.
- Mute system audio (Preferences > Dictation, `mute_system_audio`, default on, also for old config files): the default playback endpoint is muted via `IAudioEndpointVolume` once the start cue has played (immediately with sounds off) and restored before the stop cue, on stop, cancel, error, the 5 min cap and quit. Only a device that was not muted is muted, and only that device (by id) is restored; a device the user unmuted meanwhile is left alone. With "Pause media" on the order is pause, then mute; on the way out unmute, then resume. A marker file `muted-device.txt` (device id) in the data directory survives a crash and is undone at the next daemon start.

## Tray popup (daemon, `src/daemon/tray/`)
Clicking the tray icon opens a small flat popup instead of a classic menu. It is the whole UI besides the pill: Home (the menu below) and every page ("Popup pages" below). It is not glass: the design tokens above, drawn with the pill's software rasteriser and GDI text, in a layered window that exists only while it is open.
- Triggers: `NIN_SELECT` (left click), `WM_CONTEXTMENU` (right click) and `NIN_KEYSELECT` (keyboard; also from the hidden overflow area); legacy `WM_LBUTTONUP` / `WM_RBUTTONUP` when the shell refuses `NOTIFYICON_VERSION_4`. A second activation closes it; the click on the icon that merely deactivated it does not reopen it (300 ms guard); echoes of one click within 250 ms are ignored.
- Placement: card 288 px wide, 12 px from the work-area edge (all x DPI scale). Anchored on `Shell_NotifyIconGetRect` when the shell gives a rectangle that lies on the taskbar, centred on it along the bar and clamped into the work area; otherwise the corner next to the notification area (bottom-right for a bottom or right bar, top-right for a top bar, bottom-left for a left bar). The edge is read from the monitor's work area (largest inset), with `ABM_GETTASKBARPOS` for auto-hidden bars. Monitor of the icon (else of the cursor), per-monitor-v2 DPI.
- Surface: popover (`#ffffff` | `#292b30`), 1 px `border` (`#e5e5e5` | `#383b3e`), radius 10, soft shadow (0 6 20 + 0 1 3), 16 px transparent margin for it. Text `fg` / `muted-fg`, Segoe UI 13 px regular (semibold for the title and the primary row), icons drawn as vector shapes (no icon font). Rows 34 px (primary 40, recent 32), radius 6; hover = foreground overlay (5.5 % light / 8 % dark), pressed doubles it, keyboard focus a 1.75 px `primary` ring (white ring on the filled primary row). Primary row: `primary` (`#4577e9` | `#3e89ff`) fill with white text and the hotkey as key caps; `destructive` while recording; dimmed and inert while transcribing. Status dot: `success` ready, `destructive` recording, `primary` transcribing.
- Rows (top to bottom): header (logo, OpenTalk, status) / Start or Stop dictation / History / Dictionary / Settings / (Recent: up to two one-line transcript previews, click copies the full text; only with history on) / Show or Hide floating pill / Retry last dictation (only after a failure) / Quit OpenTalk. Start / Stop, Show / Hide pill, Retry and Quit run the command of the same name and close the popup; History, Dictionary and Settings navigate inside the same window to their page.
- Keyboard: Up / Down / Tab / Shift+Tab move (wrapping, disabled rows skipped), Home / End, Enter / Space choose, Esc closes. Opened from the keyboard the first row is focused. It also closes when it loses activation (a click elsewhere, Alt+Tab).
- Live state: while it is open, every dictation state change (hotkey, pill click, finished transcription, failure) refreshes it in place (status, Start / Stop row), so the row never offers the wrong action; a change that would resize the card (a Retry or Recent row appearing) closes it instead. Start / Stop is the same `Toggle` input as the hotkey, so it gets the same cues, mute, app bubble and paste target (the popup and the taskbar are ignored as targets: the app you were in before you clicked the tray).
- Cost: no timer, no thread, no window while closed; it repaints only when the highlight or the dictation state changes. Opening renders one frame (about 1 ms of CPU at 1x, text masks cached) and, for the Recent rows, reads the last 64 KB of the history file.
- `--render-tray-popup out.png [--dark] [--recording|--transcribing] [--recent] [--retry] [--pill-hidden] [--hover <row>] [--focus] [--scale s] [--transparent] [--no-text]` renders the same picture for screenshots (`docs/screenshots/tray-popup/`) and structural goldens (`tests/golden/tray_popup_*`, without text so they match everywhere). With `--page <page>` it renders a page instead (`settings`, `settings:<section>`, `history`, `entry:<id>`, `dictionary`, `snippets`, `snippet:new|<i>`, `picker:<name>`; `--height --scroll --hover --focus --edit --type --select-all --toast --confirm`), reading `OPENTALK_HOME`'s config and history when set (`docs/screenshots/tray-ui/`, `tests/golden/tray_page_*`).

### Popup pages (`src/daemon/tray/page/`)
- Routes: Home, Settings index, Settings > Preferences / Hotkeys / Speech-to-Text / Cleanup & AI / Privacy & Data / About, list pickers (language, input device, keep mic warm, retention, STT provider, AI provider, AI model), History, a history entry, Dictionary, Snippets, snippet editor. The popup keeps a back stack; the header's back arrow, Backspace (outside a field), Alt+Left and Esc pop it, and the last page returns to Home. `opentalk.exe --settings [page]` and a second launch post `WM_OT_OPEN_PAGE` (the route packed into `wparam`) to the daemon; the first run opens Settings > Speech-to-Text, a login launch nothing.
- Geometry: card 380 px wide, height `min(600, work-area height / scale - 24)` (at least 240), fixed per open so the card does not jump; the window is resized and re-placed when Home and a page swap (a bottom taskbar keeps the bottom edge). Header 48 px (back arrow, title 15 px semibold), 1 px divider, body scrolls (thin 3 px scrollbar, wheel, drag, track paging), 12 px padding above the first item and below the last, 14 px side padding. A 2.6 s status strip (toast) takes 28 px from the body while it shows.
- Items (pure `PageModel`s return them; the renderer lays out, draws and edits): group heading (11.5 px semibold, muted, 32 px), note (12 px muted, wrapped), text (13 px `fg`, wrapped: read-only content such as a transcript), banner (tone colour at 12 % with a 3 px accent bar), toggle row (36 x 20 switch), segmented control (28 px), choice row (value + chevron, opens a picker), radio card, text field (34 px box; secret, multi-line and hotkey variants; a custom editor with selection, word moves, clipboard and IME), buttons (32 px, primary / outline / destructive / link), test result, list rows (history 64 px, words 32 px, snippets 44 px; virtualised), divider, spacer. Hover wash and a `primary` focus ring as on Home; Tab / Shift+Tab move focus.
- Changes: a page edits a working copy of the config (it starts from the daemon's applied config) and posts it through an in-process mailbox (`WM_TRAY_POPUP_ACT`); the daemon applies it live (`apply_config`: hotkey, pill, theme, microphone, cues, mute, retention) and saves `config.json`, then hands the applied copy back (a rejected hotkey snaps back with a toast). API keys are saved explicitly (Save key) into the DPAPI store. The Test buttons run on a worker and report back into the open page.
- Hotkey capture: clicking the Hotkey field puts the daemon's own keyboard hook into capture mode (keys are recorded and swallowed, the Start menu stays shut, the dictation hotkey is inactive, allocation-free, 2 minute safety limit); the live combination shows in the field; Esc alone cancels; a combination that cannot be a hotkey re-arms the capture with an explanation; leaving the page or closing the popup ends it.
- Cost: as Home: no window, bitmap or timer while closed (the page caches are freed on close); an open page redraws only on input, worker results or a toast; History reads the newest 200 entries on open (more on demand) and runs whole-file searches and totals on a worker.
